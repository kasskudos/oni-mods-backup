Description
Greenhouse

A whole farm in one building. The Greenhouse grows 18 crops on three shelves of six behind glass, and its shelves follow the crop: hanging trays for ceiling crops like Pincha Pepper and Dasha Saltvine, two levels of three for trees. The Aquatic Greenhouse does the same for underwater crops in a tank of liquid. Both are 6×4 and sit in the Food menu next to the Hydroponic Farm.

The crops follow a vanilla farm's rules, but they're simulated as data once a second instead of being 18 separate plants, so a greenhouse full of crops costs almost nothing per frame. I built it with the crop simulation from my Critter Terrarium, which already handled crops, mutations and atmospheres.

Farming like a farm tile
  • One crop per greenhouse: pick it on the Config tab. Duplicants deliver the seeds and plant them; changing the crop uproots the old plants and drops their seeds. Wide plants take several slots (a Megafrond takes three).
  • Fertilizer and water: each crop uses what it uses in vanilla. Duplicants keep a few cycles' worth in stock, or you can pipe the water in and bring solids by conveyor rail. Each building takes only what it needs and lets the rest ride on, so one rail can supply a row of them.
  • Growing conditions: temperature, atmosphere, pressure, light or darkness and radiation all work as on a farm tile. If something is wrong, the Diagnostics line tells you exactly what (for example "too dark, 40 of 200 lux").
  • Gas-breathing plants: Oxyferns turn carbon dioxide into oxygen, and Alveo Vera, Megafrond and Dasha Saltvine take in their gas, from the greenhouse's own atmosphere or from the room.
  • Harvesting: Duplicants harvest ripe crops as on a farm, with the Botanist's bonus seed chance, and the harvest is dropped at the building. Turn Auto-Harvest off to keep ripe crops waiting until you press Harvest.
  • Arbor Trees: pick Arbor Tree and the shelves switch to two levels of three: 6 trees, each growing up to 5 branches that are harvested one at a time for 300 kg of Lumber, like in vanilla.
  • Bonbon Trees: the same two levels of three. Grown branches make Nectar by their light (full speed at 10,000 lux, which the glow tubes can supply), up to about 80 kg per tree per cycle. It flows out of the liquid output, or Duplicants harvest a full tree as a bottle. Branches are kept for Nectar by default; a menu toggle cuts them for Lumber instead.
  • Ovagro Node: one node fills the whole greenhouse, its vine spreading from the middle shelf to both walls, then up and down to the top and bottom shelves: 24 sections, each growing an Ovagro Fig, harvested one at a time.
  • Mimika pollination (The Prehistoric Planet Pack): turn it on and two slots grow Mimika Buds, which release their Mimikas into the greenhouse. The Mimikas pollinate the crops at the vanilla rate (+25% growth), and Sweatcorn Stalks, which wilt without pollination, can be grown. If the greenhouse is the wrong temperature or air for the buds, a small nursery closes around them for 20 W.
  • Tended or Wild: tended crops grow at full speed and need their fertilizer; wild ones grow at a quarter of the speed and need nothing, like plants in the wild.

Aquatic Greenhouse
  • A tank of liquid: pipe liquid in to fill it up to the fill level you set, like the Critter Aquarium; it's drawn inside the glass with a moving surface. The tank is the crops' water and atmosphere, and a shelf above the waterline doesn't grow.
  • Underwater crops: Waterweed, Tublia, Starnacle, Pinpoket, Clampum, and Seakomb on hanging trays.
  • Thermal Regulation of the whole tank with a built-in Liquid Tepidizer and Thermo Aquatuner (Liquid Tuning research).

Built in
Each one unlocks with the research of the vanilla building it replaces.
  • Glow tubes (Ceiling Light research): they light up only when the crop needs light, and add only what the room's light doesn't already give. They draw power for the light they add, at a Ceiling Light's rate (about 7 W for a Bristle Blossom greenhouse).
  • Farmer's Touch (Farm Station research): a Duplicant with the Farmer's Touch skill tends every crop with 5 kg of Fertilizer each, for the vanilla growth bonus. Botanists make it last longer, as in vanilla.
  • Atmosphere: pipe gases in to give the crops their own atmosphere (up to about 2 kg per tile); they're drawn inside the glass in layers, heaviest at the bottom. Or leave it open to the room.
  • Insulation (Temperature Modulation research): the inside takes the temperature of the gas piped in, and what the crops breathe out stays inside.
  • Thermal Regulation (Space Heater and Thermo Regulator research): a built-in heater and cooler keep the inside at the temperature you set.

Plant mutations (Spaced Out)
  • Choose the variant: once a mutation of your crop has been identified, pick Original, any identified variant, or one specific mutation. With one chosen, every seed of it you have is planted and the other slots grow the Original for now, replaced as more seeds arrive.
  • Mutations work as in vanilla: growth time, yield, fertilizer and water use, temperature range, light needs and harvest time follow the mutation. Bonus Lice and Rotten Heaps give their extra drops, Heavy Fruit drops its crop when ripe, and mutated plants are drawn with their vanilla look.
  • Radiation lamp: mutated plants need 250 rads. Instead of keeping Shine Bugs, the greenhouse has a radiation lamp that burns Phosphorite at the same rate Shine Bugs eat it (0.166 kg per cycle for every 60 rads), and only for the radiation the room doesn't already give. Duplicants or the rail bring the Phosphorite.
  • Mutation farm: harvests of normal plants drop seeds at the vanilla chance, and with radiation around the building each seed can come out as a new, unidentified mutation, exactly like vanilla. The Crops panel shows the chance per harvest.

Ports
Power on the left; conveyor rail, liquid and gas inputs on the right, with a liquid and a gas output just above the inputs. Gases the crops give off (Oxyfern oxygen) and liquid harvests go into the outputs when a pipe is attached. None of them are required: without pipes the crops use the room's atmosphere and Duplicants deliver everything.

Compatibility
Translated into Chinese, Korean, Russian and Japanese.

Safe to add to an existing save. Hanging Greenhouses built before 1.3.0 keep working: they're now the same building as the Greenhouse and switch their shelves the same way. If you remove the mod, the greenhouses are deleted along with their crops and stock.

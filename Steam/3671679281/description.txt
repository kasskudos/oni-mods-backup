[h1]Wireless Power – Advanced Wireless Energy Grid[/h1]

Transmit power across your base without running wires everywhere.

Wireless Power introduces a configurable wireless grid system using Batteries, Senders and Receivers, allowing clean and scalable long-distance energy distribution.

[h1]Features[/h1]

• Wireless Battery, Sender and Receiver buildings
• Channel system to isolate multiple independent wireless grids
• Configurable transfer rate
• Configurable transfer thresholds
• Automation support for Senders & Receivers
• Reduced heat output (50% lower) - NEW
• Construction uses Refined Metal - NEW
• Compatible with Base Game and Spaced Out

[h1]How It Works[/h1]

• Wireless Batteries define the total capacity of a channel
• Senders consume power locally and inject it into the selected channel
• Receivers draw power from the same channel and output it locally
• Multiple Senders/Receivers can share the same channel
• Capacity scales with the number of batteries present
• Only one transfer type (Senders OR Receivers) can exist per circuit
